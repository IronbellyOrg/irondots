# Optional compact primer shape

Prepare at most 500 words, using current approved preferences and the current routing ledger and entry's general rules. Do not replace canonical policy or copy private runtime instructions. An explicit init includes a concise prioritized agenda; the complete written briefing is separate and is not capped at 500 words.

- Current objective, approved coverage and relevant work ownership.
- Recommended first item and priority reason; its context and exact next question.
- Other ready items to take up while independent work runs.
- Applicable skill routing and source checks.
- Relevant completed/uncertain effects and approval boundaries.
- Waiting-update mode and known activity limits.
- Relevant confidentiality, reporting destination and unresolved dependency.

Omit irrelevant headings. This is a local preparation shape, not a script to recite. A safe brief acknowledgment can say that preferences loaded and name a material limitation, only after actual reads. Do not claim successful initialization from this template alone.
