# Session-init development reporting parameters

Read the canonical [routing ledger](skill-routing-ledger.md), ROUTE-04 and ROUTE-05, for all shared routing and contract locators. This module owns only session-init-specific reporting parameters, not a second routing policy.

Configure only six scoped parameters:
- Destination: existing authorized IronFlow planning record and private Space artifact category, subject to explicit project/privacy exceptions.
- Target: the independently resolved authorized session-init development record for development reporting; routine initialization has no automatic ClickUp business-task target; an already-authorized briefing update uses the resolved existing full Space report/ledger.
- Payload: finalized development version/evidence, or the reconciled source-linked briefing with freshness, coverage and remaining gaps; no raw private history.
- Acceptance: coordinator's authorized final effects and bounded readback, independently identified; no replay of completed work.
- Privacy: minimal approved preferences and relevant context, excluding secrets and confidential instructions.
- Access: one authorized coordinator owns publication through actually verified transport; preparing a skill receipt grants no write authority.

Routine initialization does not require a ClickUp write or new task. That ClickUp-specific no-op does not waive an already-authorized material update to the existing full Space briefing. For each explicit invocation, follow the unchanged fixed sequence proportionately: local reconciliation, finalized bounded payload, one authorized same-identity update, bounded verification and receipt. No ongoing progress polling or sync service is created. Read the applicable artifact skill; preserve human edits and versions. No-change requires evidence and avoids unnecessary versions. Missing authority/access or failed intended persistence is pending, never successful no-op. A purely conversational scope with no requested persistence may justify no-op explicitly.
