# Handoff reconciliation
Inputs: versioned batch, source captures, result claims and actual provider evidence. Outputs: validated local import, conflicts and final per-effect manifest. No implicit external writes.

Read batch-contract. Validate schema, IDs, hashes, scope, source versions, audience, approval and receipt correlations before import. Keep original raw captures and source-reported claims separate from independently observed receipts. Compare only relevant fields using both value and field presence; preserve disjoint human edits. Relevant drift produces needs_decision, never last-write-wins. Null differs from omitted; 404/access loss/moved scope differs from deletion. Additive unknown fields stay raw; identity/type-breaking schema drift blocks dependent interpretation/effects.

Use helper conflict checks and receipt validation. A successful transport receipt is not delivery/read proof. Receipt evidence must include provider ID, target/action identity, source-read route and observed_at; independently corroborate exact payload/audience/effect before verified. Unknown/uncertain effect cannot be blindly replayed. Source-reported outcomes lacking evidence remain unverified regardless of confident prose.

Finish work in its environment, then transfer one finalized manifest through authoritative reporting-protocol-v1.md and exact request/result templates. Complete, partial, failed and cancelled handoffs are distinct. Necessary intervention/approval/urgent blockers may surface earlier. Partial handoff retains pending ownership, blockers and next steps; it does not abandon unresolved authorized work. Replace the established Space identity only after conflict/audience checks. No unchanged duplicate version; failed reads retain last good with explicit freshness gaps. Do not export private raw chats into shared reports.

Executable handoff schema is `dot-batch-handoff/0.1`: id, batch_id, status, scope_revision, action_states, generated_at, source_versions, receipt_ids, unresolved, next_step and audience. Freeze action_states at handoff creation; Store.save checks new snapshots against the entire current batch. Each nonverified/noncancelled/nonskipped action needs an unresolved entry with action_id, owner, blocker and next_step. Historical snapshots remain immutable as later scope revisions add work. Every claimed verified effect needs a correlated receipt; validated structure still does not authenticate external evidence.
