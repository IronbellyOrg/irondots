# Bounded debate

Load after preflight. Inputs: frozen variants, criteria, sources and allowed effects. Output: source-linked issue ledger and actual advocate records. No publication or new authority.

## Issue ledger

Assign stable IDs: S structural, C substantive content, X contradictions, U unique contributions, A shared assumptions. Preserve IDs across revisions. Each issue records exact source/section, falsifiable claim or preference, impact, classification, applicability and resolution. Classify factual material as observed, source-reported, inferred, proposed or unknown; a user decision is distinct from a factual claim.

Extract agreement's hidden preconditions as well as visible disagreements. Mark assumptions stated, unstated, contradicted or unknown. Promote material unstated/contradicted assumptions for explicit ACCEPT, REJECT or QUALIFY responses with evidence/conditions. Similar artifacts still need this check; cosmetic duplication never excuses scrutiny of a shared false premise. Empty categories need no invented findings.

Classify applicable concerns by meaning: surface, structure, state/behavior mechanics. Record irrelevant levels as not applicable with reasons. Do not force an issue into each level or add unbounded rounds to satisfy taxonomy.

## Role packet and rounds

Follow [isolation](isolation.md). Start every role with a fresh no-history agent. Give each advocate the same frozen original-source/criteria packet and all variants, plus its assigned perspective. Keep the coordinator’s source-derived issue ledger out of the first statements; after all first statements are retained, distribute the same current-run issue ledger and completed statements for rebuttal. Do not pass prior-run results or the parent’s evaluative summaries. Do not leak desired conclusions. Require these concise outputs:
1. Position and strongest opposing argument before critique.
2. Strengths and criticisms tied to exact evidence.
3. Genuine concessions and what evidence would change its position.
4. Disposition of every assigned material A-ID and unresolved issue.

Quick: one independent statement per advocate, then adjudicate. Normal: statements and one rebuttal round. Deep: normal plus at most one targeted final round for remaining material issues. Rebuttals receive the same completed prior-round packet, not an accumulating advantage based on speaking order. Schedule in waves if needed; do not simulate missing agents.

Log exact packet identities, disclosure barriers and reads as well as actual dispatch/results with role and stage identity. Flag omissions. Repair a malformed contribution only within the declared effort/round bounds. Retry a recoverable known-not-completed internal operation only within those bounds; inspect uncertain outcomes before any retry. After a denial, retry only on materially new relevant evidence and within policy. If fewer than two advocates survive, return partial/failed and the available work without calling it completed debate.

## Agreement and stopping

Freeze eligible issue IDs for each comparison. Include material assumptions and unique contributions promoted to decision points; explicitly exclude duplicates, cosmetic/non-applicable items. An issue is agreed only if every expected advocate explicitly supports the same disposition and conditions. A split, abstention or missing response is not agreement. Do not shrink the expected roster to inflate agreement. The ratio is agreed IDs divided by eligible IDs, null for an empty denominator.

Agreement never establishes truth. Stop at covered scope, the configured final round, a real limit, cancellation or blocker. Preserve unresolved disagreement and critical unknowns. No forced winner, threshold-based waiver or claim of convergence from an unperformed check. A capacity failure, omitted required round or missing required reviewer remains visible in the result.
