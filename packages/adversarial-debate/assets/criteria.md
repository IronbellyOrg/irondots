# Criteria registry guidance

Choose the smallest set that decides the user's question. Record each ID, description, criticality, applicability, source/rationale and evidence needed before judging. Typical dimensions are requirement coverage, correctness, feasibility, dependencies, clarity and material risks/invariants. They are prompts, not mandatory criterion counts.

Evaluate with claim, evidence, verdict and rationale. Unknown differs from failed; not-applicable needs a reason. Critical unknown/failed gates prevent unconditional selection. If requested scores are used, keep the full applicable denominator visible, including unknown criteria. Do not reward verbosity, copied requirement IDs, invented specificity, or majority agreement.

Counterexamples: a brief factual choice need not have three invented risks; a plan with a shared invalid precondition is not valid because both advocates agree; a no-applicable-criteria case has no meaningful normalized score.
