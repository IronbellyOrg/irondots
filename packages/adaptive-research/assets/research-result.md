# Research result

Use only when a structured report is requested. Populate from actual evidence; omit irrelevant presentation headings, never fabricate missing content. Skill callers still receive all fields in research-result/1.0.

## Answer and decision
Direct answer, with recommendations distinguished from findings.

## Scope and depth
Requested/selected mode; separate complexity and importance; included/excluded scope and finite plan.

## Evidence and coverage
Consequential claims with supporting source locations; source versions/dates and inspected scope; question coverage and explicit gaps.

## Contradictions and limitations
Material conflicts, applicability, uncertainty and what could change the answer.

## Execution and validation
Actual resources/modules/tools used, conditional skips, observed effort and unavailable telemetry, checks performed and tests not performed.

## Result and next step
Research state and stopping reason; bounded next action and authorization needs; separate publication state/verified receipt only if requested.
